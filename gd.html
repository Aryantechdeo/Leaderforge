🧠 Group Discussion Practice

▶ Personality vs Academic Excellence

FOR Personality:

  • Values guide knowledge.
  • Good character builds trust.

FOR Academics:

  • Knowledge creates opportunities.
  • Strong academics build expertise.

Conclusion: Both are important, but personality determines how knowledge is used.

▶Discipline Through Fear or Freedom

Fear may create short-term discipline.

Freedom encourages self-discipline and responsibility.

▶Democratic vs Authoritarian Leadership

Democratic leadership encourages participation.

Authoritarian leadership allows faster decisions.

▶Character Building vs Academic Excellence

Character shapes behavior.

Academics shape knowledge.
